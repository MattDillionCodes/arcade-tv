# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

First public release.

- Start screen with live weather and clock tiles, a hero tile and your favourite apps, which
  resize (small, medium, wide), recolour, move and pin.
- Apps list, A to Z under letter headings.
- Action Center: quick settings, volume, HDMI inputs, 20 accent colours, solid or photo tiles,
  wallpaper, weather city and °F / °C, screensaver timer.
- Send wallpapers from your phone by QR code; six wallpapers built in.
- Lock-screen screensaver with the clock, date and weather.
- 12- or 24-hour clock, following the TV's setting.
